The pain points

Legal help often starts with uncertainty.

People know they need help, but not always what kind of help, which lawyer to approach, what documents matter, or how to follow progress after a case begins.

01

Where do I start?

Legal issues are hard to classify, especially across civil, criminal, family, land, and cheque matters.

02

Who can I trust?

Finding the right lawyer by district, expertise, fee, and availability takes time and confidence.

03

What is happening?

Case progress, hearing dates, document status, and next steps are often scattered or unclear.

04

How fast can I talk?

People need practical advice quickly, without arranging multiple calls just to understand the path.

The Mokoddoma response

From legal friction to a guided mobile pathway.

Mokoddoma is not trying to replace lawyers. It helps people reach the right service faster, with better context, clearer communication, and a workflow designed around Bangladesh.

What we provide

Four services, one connected experience.

Case coverage

Built around the legal categories people actually search for.

Mokoddoma organizes matters into practical categories so users can explain their issue quickly and lawyers can understand the request with better context.

Criminal

Murder, assault, theft, robbery, fraud, narcotics, cheque dishonor, and related matters.

Civil and Land

Property disputes, land mutation, partition suits, contracts, money recovery, and eviction.

Family

Divorce, khula, child custody, maintenance, inheritance, and family-related legal questions.

Other Legal Issues

A flexible pathway for issues that do not fit neatly into one category but still need legal attention.
