Case coverage

Support across common legal categories

The Mukadma app organizes matters by practical categories so clients can explain their issue and lawyers can understand the nature of the request quickly.

Criminal

Murder, assault, theft, robbery, fraud, narcotics, cheque dishonor, and related matters.

Civil

Property disputes, land mutation, partition suits, contract disputes, money recovery, and eviction.

Family

Divorce, khula, child custody, maintenance, inheritance, and family-related legal questions.

Other Legal Issues

A pathway for matters that do not fit neatly into one category but still need legal attention.
